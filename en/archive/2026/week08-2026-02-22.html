The week in brief

Week 8/2026 followed a clear trend: top performance is becoming more accessible, and tools are becoming more precisely specialised. The biggest event was the release of Claude Sonnet 4.6, which brings Opus-level intelligence at Sonnet prices and resets the price-performance ratio for AI models. Moda addresses one of the biggest frustrations with AI design tools: the lack of editability. Boost.space v5 tackles a fundamental problem for companies working with several AI agents through its "shared context" approach. Figr AI and Clawi.ai complete the week with specialised solutions for UX research and broader access to AI agents.

  • Claude Sonnet 4.6: Opus intelligence at Sonnet prices (9.5/10)
  • Trend: performance becomes accessible, tools become more specialised
