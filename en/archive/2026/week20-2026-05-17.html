The week in brief

The most interesting development this week is that AI tools connect context and execution. Shadow handles meeting follow-ups during the call, Kanwas organises product knowledge for people and agents, and Mindra builds operational agent teams from it. Aaavatar shows that data protection can be a real advantage in design tools when processing is local-first. AnySearch makes clear that good agents need better access to information, not only better models.

  • Aaavatar and Shadow (8/10): Aaavatar sets a strong data protection benchmark with local-first portrait editing, Shadow moves meeting AI from notes to real execution.
  • Trend: from isolated prompts to context-aware work systems. AI tools move towards real-time execution, team knowledge, agentic research and operational orchestration.
  • Shift: from all-in-one to specialised solutions
