The week in brief

Week 14/2026 marks a landmark week for open-source AI: Google released Gemma 4 under the Apache 2.0 licence for the first time, a shift that directly challenges proprietary cloud models. H Company's Holo3 sets a new standard for computer-use agents with 78.85% on the OSWorld benchmark, ahead of GPT-5.4 and Claude Opus 4.6, as a European company under Apache 2.0. In data protection, Atomic Chat stands out: the open-source tool runs completely offline on the Mac, without a single byte leaving the device. The week is rounded off by Noon leaving stealth mode with 44 million dollars for the first AI-native design tool on production code, and by Perplexity Computer's new tax module for US tax returns.

  • Google Gemma 4 (9.5/10): a milestone, frontier open-source AI under Apache 2.0, from Raspberry Pi to H100, fully GDPR-compliant on-premise
  • Trend: open source overtakes cloud AI. Gemma 4 (Apache 2.0) and Holo3 (Apache 2.0, EU) show that near-frontier AI quality is now available on-premise, free and without licence risk
  • Shift: from all-in-one to specialised solutions
