The week in brief

Week 13 of 2026 shows a strong trend towards autonomous agents and their safe integration into existing workflows. While Anthropic enables AI to control computers directly with "Claude Computer Use", platforms such as Agentplace provide the infrastructure to deploy such agents with clear guardrails. In marketing, tools like Pendium shift the focus from classic SEO to "AI visibility", so that brands appear in the answers of AI models. In design, Omma by Spline sets new standards by using parallel agents to create 3D worlds and interactive websites.
