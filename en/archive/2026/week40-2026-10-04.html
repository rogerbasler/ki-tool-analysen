The week in brief

This week AI becomes valuable where an answer turns into controllable execution. Claude Sonnet 5.5 speeds up clearly bounded knowledge work, while Strands Decider 2B carries out small routine decisions locally and with a confidence score. Suno Speech lowers the barrier for audio prototypes, Slashspace connects research in a local canvas and Dots shows the potential of persistent cloud agents. Data flows, permissions, regional availability and human approval remain decisive.

  • Claude Sonnet 5.5 (8.8/10): strong for clearly bounded work, but only with a clean data and review process.
  • Trend: from the single answer to controllable execution with local decisions, permissions and escalations.
  • Rule of thumb: start with test data, minimal permissions, measurable quality criteria and a human approval point.
