The week in brief

Week 18 shows a clear trend towards deep integration of AI agents into existing workflows. Genspark for Word brings capable AI directly into Microsoft 365, while Buda offers a complete platform for building an AI company. TrafficClaw rethinks web analytics with an AI chat interface, and Bitgrain positions itself as a lightweight, flexible design alternative. Anthropic also greatly extends Claude's creative options with new connectors. Trend of the week: agentic workflows inside standard tools. AI leaves isolated chat interfaces and is embedded directly in Microsoft Office, design software and analytics dashboards.

  • Genspark for Word (9/10) and Buda (9/10): AI agents integrate seamlessly into everyday work and take on complex, multi-step tasks.

Trend: Agentic workflows in standard tools: AI leaves isolated chat interfaces and is embedded directly in Microsoft Office, design software and analytics dashboards.
