The week in brief

Week 11/2026 marks the move from general AI assistants to vertical work tools with clear ROI logic. Naoma AI shows how AI agents step directly into the sales process and turn website traffic into personalised product demos. Citable makes a new growth vector visible: the presence of brands in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and other AI systems. OrangeLabs opens up access to data, documents and web research through an affordable analysis workspace. Teract professionalises building personal visibility across several networks, and Canva Magic Layers shows how established platforms integrate AI as a natural extension of the existing creative workflow rather than an add-on.

  • Naoma AI: strongest tool of the week, an AI video sales agent with clear revenue logic and solid data protection communication (8.8/10)
  • Trend: vertical AI tools displace generalists; the winners are products with direct impact on revenue or productivity
  • Market signal: data protection transparency becomes a real product feature in 2026, especially for DACH teams and B2B procurement
